Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pokren ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Data Collection

Pokren does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All app data is stored locally on your device.

Information Stored Locally

The following data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Training progress and statistics (scores, accuracy, streaks)
  • Drill session history and review records
  • Achievement progress and level data
  • App preferences and settings (daily goal, etc.)

Third-Party Services

Pokren does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with any third party.

Data Security

Your data is stored using iOS standard secure storage mechanisms (UserDefaults with device encryption). Since no data leaves your device, there is no risk of network-based data exposure.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by using the "Reset Progress" feature in the Profile tab, or by uninstalling the app. Deleting the app removes all stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy

Pokren is a general-audience educational tool focused on strategy training. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts, social features, or age-restricted content.

No Gambling

Pokren is strictly an educational tool for learning card strategy concepts and decision-making skills. It does not involve real money, simulated currency, or any form of wagering. There are no in-app purchases related to chance-based outcomes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our support page.
