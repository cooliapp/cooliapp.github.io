Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Profile tab → scroll down → tap "Reset Progress". This will clear all statistics, achievements, and review history.

Can I change my daily training goal?

Yes. Navigate to Profile → Settings → Daily Goal. You can set any target number of questions per day.

Is my data backed up?

Your progress is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud backup enabled, your app data may be included in your device backup.

Does the app use real money?

No. Pokren is a purely educational training tool. There is no real money, virtual currency, or any form of wagering involved. All content is focused on learning card game strategy and decision-making skills.

How does the difficulty system work?

Drills range from Level 1 (basic hand recognition) to Level 5 (advanced strategy). Start at Level 1 and progress as your accuracy improves.

What are XP and Levels?

You earn Experience Points (XP) for correct answers. Accumulating enough XP advances your level, which tracks your overall growth.

The app is not loading properly

Try force-closing the app and restarting it. If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall. Note that reinstalling will reset your progress.
