Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Overview

VeloraCare ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our interdental care tracking application.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally

All data you enter into VeloraCare is stored exclusively on your device. This includes:

  • Flossing session records (date, time, duration, technique)
  • Area coverage information
  • Comfort and sensitivity notes
  • Personal goals and preferences

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers. Specifically:

  • No personal identification information
  • No location data
  • No health data shared with third parties
  • No analytics or tracking services
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No cookies or web tracking

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. We do not use cloud sync, iCloud backup integration, or any remote database.

If you delete the app, all data stored within the app will be permanently removed from your device.

Data Export

VeloraCare provides a data export feature that allows you to save your records in CSV format. This export is initiated only by you and produces a file stored on your device. We have no access to exported files.

Third-Party Services

VeloraCare does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The app operates entirely offline and does not make network requests.

Children's Privacy

VeloraCare does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences interested in oral care habit tracking.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
