Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a new flossing session?

Open the app and tap the "Start New Session" button on the Ritual tab. You can use the built-in timer or manually enter your session duration. Follow the guided steps to record your technique, areas covered, and comfort level.

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or sync your information to any external server. Your records remain private and accessible only to you.

How do I export my records?

Go to Settings > Data > Export Data. Your records will be exported as a CSV file that you can save, share, or open in any spreadsheet application.

Can I change my daily goal?

Yes! Navigate to Settings > Daily Goal. You can adjust the number of target sessions per day (1-5) and configure reminder notifications.

What does the streak counter track?

The streak counter shows the number of consecutive days you've completed at least one flossing session. Missing a day will reset the counter, encouraging consistent daily habits.

How do I delete a session record?

In the Journal tab, find the session you want to remove, swipe left on the record card, and tap Delete. Please note that deleted records cannot be recovered.

The app is not responding. What should I do?

Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If the issue persists, restart your device. If you continue experiencing problems, please contact us using the email below.
