Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Your privacy matters. Brivora is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not collect, store, or share your personal information with any third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Brivora does not collect any personal data. All information you enter into the app, including brushing records, habit data, and preferences, is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms.

  • No account required: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app.
  • No analytics: We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking services.
  • No advertising: The app does not display advertisements and does not share data with advertisers.

2. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device.
  • Data is automatically removed when you uninstall the app.
  • You can reset all data at any time through the Settings screen within the app.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

To be clear, Brivora does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Health data beyond what you manually enter
  • Photos, contacts, or any other device data

4. Third-Party Services

Brivora does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The app functions entirely offline and does not require an internet connection for its core features.

5. Children's Privacy

Brivora does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is designed for general audiences and can be safely used by users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
