Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a brushing session?

Open the app and tap "Start Brushing Timer" on the Today tab. The timer will guide you through each quadrant of your mouth for a thorough 2-minute session.

Can I log a past brushing session?

Yes! Tap "Log Manual Entry" on the Today tab to record a session that happened earlier. You can set the date, time, duration, and all other details.

How is my quality score calculated?

Your quality score is based on multiple factors: brushing duration (target 2 minutes), zone coverage, pressure level, flossing, and mouthwash usage. Each factor contributes to a score from 0 to 100.

Where is my data stored?

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not send any data to external servers. Your brushing records and habits are completely private.

How do I set up reminders?

Go to Settings and toggle on Morning Reminder or Evening Reminder. You can customize the exact time for each reminder to match your routine.

How do I add a new habit?

Navigate to the Routine tab and tap the "+" button in the top right corner. Choose a name, category, and frequency for your new habit.

Can I reset my data?

Yes, go to Settings and scroll to the bottom. Tap "Reset All Data" to clear all records and start fresh. Please note this action cannot be undone.

What do the insight charts show?

The Insights tab provides visual breakdowns of your brushing quality over time, duration trends, session distribution, pressure patterns, and care habit rates (flossing and mouthwash).
