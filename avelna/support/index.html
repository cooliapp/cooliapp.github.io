Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a training session?

Tap the "+" button on the Balance tab. Fill in your session details including duration, rotations, calories, intensity level, and mood. Tap "Save" to record your session.

How do I set up daily routines?

Navigate to the Routine tab and tap "+". Give your routine a name, set a time, choose a category (Warm Up, Training, Cool Down, Stretch, or Rest), select repeat days, and enable reminders if desired.

Can I view my progress over time?

Yes! The Insights tab shows your training trends over the past week, month, or three months. You can view calories burned, session duration, and intensity breakdown through visual charts.

How do I change my weekly goals?

Go to Settings and adjust the "Sessions per week" target using the stepper control. Your progress on the Balance tab will update to reflect the new goal.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored locally on your device. It is included in your standard iCloud or iTunes device backups. We recommend keeping regular device backups.

How do I reset all data?

Go to Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Reset All Data." This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
