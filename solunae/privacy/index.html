Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Solunae respects your privacy. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information We Collect

Solunae does not collect any personal information. All habit tracking data, settings, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using local storage.

Data Storage

Your data is saved locally on your iPhone and is not transmitted to any external servers. This includes:

  • Sun exposure session records
  • Nap and rest period logs
  • Energy level entries
  • App settings and preferences
  • Streak and progress data

Third-Party Services

Solunae does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data leaves your device.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the app's Settings page using the "Reset All Data" option. Uninstalling the app will also permanently remove all stored data from your device.

Children's Privacy

Solunae does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
