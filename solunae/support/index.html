Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a session?

Navigate to the Pulse tab and tap the + button in the top right corner. Fill in your session details including duration, intensity, and body area.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device. If you back up your iPhone through iCloud or iTunes, your app data will be included in that backup.

How do I reset my data?

Go to Settings and scroll down to the Data section. Tap "Reset All Data" to permanently remove all records. This action cannot be undone.

Can I change my daily goal?

Yes, go to Settings and adjust the Daily Goal slider to set your preferred target between 10 minutes and 2 hours.

How does the streak work?

Your streak increases by one each day you log at least one session. If you miss a day, the streak resets. Your longest streak is always recorded in the Progress tab.

The app is not working properly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, please contact our support team with details about the problem.
